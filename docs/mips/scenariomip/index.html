    ScenarioMIP

    ScenarioMIP (Scenario Model Intercomparison Project) carries out Earth system model simulations driven by alternative plausible futures of emissions and land use. These include a set of scenarios, intended to be run in emission-driven mode (GMD, 2026), exploring high levels of climate change (to explore high-end climate risks), medium levels of climate change (anchored to current policy), and low levels of climate change (aligned with current international agreements). This also includes plans for long-term extensions to study long-term impacts, climate change-related processes on long timescales and the (ir)reversibility of climate change.
